# Support

## Where to ask

- **Bug reports and feature requests**: open an issue at
  <https://github.com/songoao25/obsidian-knowledge-vault/issues>. Include your OS and version,
  what you expected, what actually happened, and the exact command you ran.
- **Security problems**: do **not** open a public issue — follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Before opening a pull request**: read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md); it lists the privacy
  gate and release checks that must pass.

## What to include

- The OS you tested on and the version of the released package (not just the source checkout).
- The command you ran and its full output.
- Whether the problem reproduces from a clean checkout.

## Not in scope

- Recovering notes that were overwritten outside this tool, or repairing a vault damaged by
  another application.
- **Never** attach real notes, vault exports, logs, screenshots, or credentials to an issue.
